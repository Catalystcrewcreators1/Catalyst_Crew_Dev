Basic Details: Event Name: Catalyst Hack 2026 Starts On: 25th September, 2026 Registration Ends On: 20th October, 2026 Mode: Fully Online Organizer: Catalyst Crew Dev Community Team Size: Minimum 4 – Maximum 5 Members Overview Catalyst Hack 2026 is a fully online technology and innovation hackathon designed to turn meaningful ideas into practical, scalable, and impactful solutions. The event brings together student developers, innovators, designers, and technology enthusiasts to solve real-world problems through collaboration and hands-on building. Teams progress through an online PPT and idea screening round, a mentor-screening semi-final, and an 8-hour final hackathon. Shortlisted teams present their ideas, explain technical feasibility, and build working prototypes for final evaluation. • Round 1 - Online PPT & Idea Submission: Teams submit a concise presentation covering the problem, target users, proposed solution, innovation, technology stack, impact, feasibility, and roadmap. • Round 2 - Semi-Final: Shortlisted teams participate in an online mentor screening involving a solution pitch, technical discussion, feasibility, scalability, and execution-readiness assessment. Participation fee is ₹80 per member. • Round 3 - Final Hackathon: Selected finalist teams participate in an 8-hour online build sprint and submit a working prototype, repository, documentation, and final presentation. • Adherence to Standards: Participants must follow the event rules, submit required deliverables on time, and maintain originality and professional conduct throughout the competition. The final winners will be selected by the judging panel based on innovation, technical implementation, practical impact, scalability, prototype quality, and presentation.Timeline 25 September 2026 ->
